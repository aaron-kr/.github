# Aaron.kr

Aaron Snowberger ([aaron.kr](https://aaron.kr)) is a Ph.D. holder in Information & Communications Engineering from Hanbat National University, South Korea, specializing in computer vision, machine learning, and natural language processing. With over a decade of experience teaching technology and computer science, Aaron has lectured at multiple universities, including Jeonju University, Jeonbuk University, and Jeonju National University of Education.

In addition to his academic achievements, Aaron has a strong background in web development, graphic design, and public speaking, with a passion for turning complex ideas into clear, impactful solutions. He has developed websites and mobile apps for various businesses, including KOTESOL. He is also a Google Certified Educator and Trainer, with experience designing educational resources and providing training seminars across South Korea. Aaron's research interests focus on technology-driven solutions for education and ancient script recognition. He thrives on integrating cutting-edge technologies with practical teaching methodologies to make learning more efficient and engaging.
